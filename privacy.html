Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Channel Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and set their thumbnails.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to check whether those videos are scheduled or live, and read view, like and comment counts.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read watch-time figures for the owner's own videos.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and produce reports for the owner. Video IDs and view numbers are stored in the owner's private repository. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets and are never shared, sold or used for advertising.

No data from other YouTube users is collected. Channel Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube's own terms apply: youtube.com/t/terms. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
